About ABU Delivery

ABU CAMPUS DELIVERY is a student-focused delivery business designed to make buying, selling, ordering, and receiving products easier for university students.

We started around Jimma University with a simple idea: walking-based delivery. We connect students with shops, small businesses, and student vendors — you place the order, we do the walking.

Our Promise: Fast, convenient, and affordable. We are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, serving Jimma University and all accessible surrounding campus areas.

"Your Campus. Your Orders. Delivered."
